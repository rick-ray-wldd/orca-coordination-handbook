# Decision record

> Ask one clear decision at a time. Provide concrete options.

```text
Decision: <D-EXAMPLE-00> | <question>
Task: <T-EXAMPLE0000> | Proposed by: <supervisor>
Raised: <date/time> | Needed by: <date, target or confirmed_deadline>
Impact if unanswered: <...>
Context: <up to three sentences>
Option 1: <scope, benefit, cost/risk, external action or spending?>
Option 2: <scope, benefit, cost/risk, external action or spending?>
Recommendation: <option and reason>
User decision (fill only after confirmation):
- Choice: <...>
- Original source and time: <...>
- Additional authorization: <none / exact action and scope>
```

Authorization covers the stated action only. After the answer, update next/state and any changed routing/dispatch scope. Silence is not an answer.
